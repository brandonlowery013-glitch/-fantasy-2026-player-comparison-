Weekly publication requirements

- Prepare projections and other weekly material Tuesday using the available evidence. Missing inputs remain identified rather than invented.
- Complete original team matchup write-ups Wednesday using the information available then.
- Later availability, lineup and material matchup developments update the relevant explanation and timestamp. A Wednesday write-up is not treated as a final injury report.
- Freeze the issued pregame prediction and its original supporting evidence for learning; subsequent revisions retain their own timestamps.
- An automatic data refresh preserves the reader's category, selected matchup, open disclosures, filters, focus and reading position. Each category owns its content; the default weekly renderer cannot overwrite Role Watch or Player Outlook.
- This records publication requirements. It does not alter schedule YAML or authorize rerunning broad collectors.
