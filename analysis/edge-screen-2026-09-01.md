## Interpretation rules

1. Market gaps do not rewrite True Value by themselves.
2. BUY means exploit the discount at or after the model target range, not draft the player at the model rank automatically.
3. REACH/FADE means wait for the saved fair range; it is not a statement that the player is bad.
4. Before any rank mutation, a football-evidence trigger must reopen the intrinsic evaluation.
5. This screen should feed the comparison engine as an independent edge layer so consensus and the internal model can disagree visibly rather than collapse into one signal.
