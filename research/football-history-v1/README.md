# Football history and moneyline research — October 25, 2026

This module and candidate packet are research only. Nothing in the live app imports them. Live moneyline/spread/totals/prop models were not replaced.

The history separates confirmed starter changes, injury exits, confirmed returns, actual participation, and benching speculation. Every event has a source and an available-at time; game-specific availability does not become a permanent absence. Role and observed workload are kept separate from an unvalidated injury point value.

The pure inference function reproduces the selected research moneyline fit from two-sided moneyline odds and pregame team/QB inputs. It has no live authority, no team-specific probability overrides, no quota of underdog picks, and no fallback to an experimental model.

## Limits

These seasons have been repeatedly inspected. This is exploratory historical replay, not proof of a future win rate. Weekly injury reports lack exact publication times; dated depth snapshots support a near-kickoff reconstruction. Older seasons do not have the same complete expected-lineup archive in this run. Some best-fit coefficients are counterintuitive conditional associations; they are not causal claims about player value. The constrained comparison is retained in the candidate packet. The sample does not justify automatic production promotion.

## Learning contract

Keep the chosen family fixed. On a weekly update, reconcile sources and use only earlier settled outcomes, with prior-game performance assigned to the QB who actually played. Do not infer a new starter from a replacement's box-score volume, turn speculation into confirmation, or let an old injury flag persist after verified clearance. Save a prediction before kickoff so later corrections do not become past predictions.

No paid subscriptions or historical-odds purchases were used. Firefox was not touched.
