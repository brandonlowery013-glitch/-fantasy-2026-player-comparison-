Dallas brings the reputation. Indianapolis brings the points.

The scoring model has this almost level at 24.8–24.7. Dallas may have the louder fanbase, but volume is not a predictive feature. All three lean Indy +4.5; the two calibration variants refuse to turn that lean into a pick.

NYJ @ KC

Nine and a half points: the Jets loyalty program.

All three approve New York +9.5. This is a cover forecast, not a declaration that the Jets have become trustworthy. You can lose the game and still leave the sportsbook looking employed.

Miami gets a cushion, not a character reference.

CLE @ NO

NYG @ PHI

LA @ WAS

DEN @ CAR

HOU @ LAC

GB @ NE

LV @ SF

ARI @ SEA

TB @ CHI
