The matchup roast

CIN @ ATL

The early game has already clocked in.

The early game had already started when this dispatch was prepared. No new pregame pick is created after kickoff. The comedy department has a keyboard, not a time machine.

DAL @ IND

Dallas brings the reputation. Indianapolis brings the points.

Dallas brings the national spotlight; Indianapolis brings a fanbase asking whether the spotlight can also locate a defense. Brandon has already denied the Colts a character reference. The game remains on the schedule anyway.

NYJ @ KC

Nine and a half points: the Jets loyalty program.

The Jets travel to Kansas City hoping the underdog role comes with complimentary snacks. Winning and covering are different assignments; New York would appreciate partial credit.

DET @ MIA

Miami gets a cushion, not a character reference.

Detroit arrives with ambition. Miami arrives with humidity and the hope that the visitors packed the wrong shoes. Someone is leaving this game with an explanation that begins with “if you take away those two plays.”

CLE @ NO

The committee has elected to do absolutely nothing.

Cleveland and New Orleans meet in a game whose postgame press conference may contain more promises than the action. Somebody has to win, unless both offenses negotiate an extension.

NYG @ PHI

An NFC East disagreement: shocking development.

An NFC East game: four quarters of football followed by six days of people arguing like the result settled a property dispute. The winner gets bragging rights; the loser gets a very loud local radio segment.

LA @ WAS

The Rams–Washington file stays on the desk.

Los Angeles visits Washington for a game that will have one fanbase declaring the season back on and the other pricing coordinator replacements. Both reactions can happen before halftime.

DEN @ CAR

Carolina is asking for a little respect and a few points.

Denver and Carolina meet with every fan convinced this is the game their team should handle. That sentence is responsible for approximately half of Sunday’s emotional damage.

HOU @ LAC

The Chargers are receiving points and your emotional baggage.

Houston and the Chargers have been assigned a football game. Chargers fans have also been assigned a breathing exercise and a reminder not to celebrate before the clock reaches zero.

GB @ NE

The models have requested separate hotel rooms.

Green Bay and New England bring enough history to open a museum. Unfortunately for both teams, the scoreboard does not accept championships from previous decades as legal tender.

LV @ SF

The Raiders: a large cushion with no warranty.

Las Vegas visits San Francisco, where optimism will be inspected at the door. The Raiders would like a competitive afternoon; their fans would settle for an afternoon that does not require an apology.

ARI @ SEA

Seattle’s résumé meets Arizona’s head start.

Arizona travels to Seattle for the traditional divisional reminder that familiarity does not produce friendship. One fanbase will call it a statement win; the other will announce that division games are always weird.

TB @ CHI

Chicago weather, prime time, and a six-point argument.

Tampa Bay comes to Chicago for prime time, giving Bears fans a national audience for feelings they were going to have anyway. The weather, play-calling and offensive line are all available for blame.

JAX @ BAL

Thursday has left the group chat.

Thursday’s game is already final in the verified schedule. It stays in the full-week roll call, but any brand-new “pregame lock” for this one deserves a fraud investigation.

BUF @ MIN

Minnesota gets three points and three signatures.

Buffalo and Minnesota close the slate with two fanbases who understand that hope should come with a waiver. Somebody gets a satisfying Monday night. Somebody gets to stare at the ceiling.
